Acme Corp Press Release

Acme Corp today announced general availability of the Acme Knowledge Platform 4.0. The release adds incremental ingestion, per-collection vector store selection, and a new evaluation suite measuring recall@k and MRR against customer-supplied golden sets.

Highlights

  • Per-collection embedding provider (OpenAI, Cohere, Voyage, custom).
  • Resumable uploads up to 100 GB per session.
  • Signed webhook deliveries with retry and circuit-breaker.

Pricing starts at $499/month for the Team tier and scales to a six-figure Enterprise agreement with on-premise support.
